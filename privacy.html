Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

What we collect

  • Account data from your sign-in provider (e.g. Google): an account ID, email address and display name.
  • Game data: characters, progress, items, chat messages in the online town.
  • Purchase records: what you bought, when, and the amount. Card and payment details are handled by Stripe and are never stored by us.
  • Discord user ID, if you link Discord for supporter perks.

How we use it

To run your account and the game, deliver purchases, prevent cheating and fraud, and answer support requests. We do not sell your personal data and we do not show third-party ads.

Service providers

Supabase (accounts and database), Stripe (payments), Fly.io (game servers), GitHub Pages and Cloudflare (website hosting), Discord (community perks).

Your choices

You can ask us to export or delete your account and data by emailing contact@l0ginx.app. We keep purchase records only as long as required by law.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13. Players under 18 should have a parent's permission before making a purchase.
